Board management

Instructions

  1. Create a new board and give it a name and the names of the teams playing.
  2. Once created, click Open Board to view or share the link to the board.
  3. Once all the squares are selected, come back here and populate the random numbers when the board is ready.
  4. After each quarter, come back here and enter the score for both teams.
  5. Lock the board before kickoff to prevent further selections.
Pro tip: create an offline version
  1. Create a new board.
  2. Populate the random numbers.
  3. Print the board, but don't reveal the random numbers. Fold over those columns.
  4. Have everyone write their initials in the squares they select.
  5. Reveal the random numbers at kickoff.
