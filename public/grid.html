Instructions

The goal is to match the last digit of the score for each team to the numbers assigned to your square.

  1. Reserve a square by clicking an available X in the grid.
  2. Only the person who reserved a square can cancel it.
  3. Random numbers represent the last digit of each team's score and are generated at kickoff.
  4. For each quarter and the final, the square with the right Team A and Team B score combination is the winner.
  5. Payouts are 20% for each quarter and 40% for the final.
